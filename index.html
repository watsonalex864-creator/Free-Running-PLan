Why Running More Isn't The Answer

Most free running plans give you one lever: run more, run faster, or both, and hope your body keeps up. That's how you plateau — or get injured. The runners who keep improving year after year aren't just running well; they're building the tissue capacity and movement quality to handle the training in the first place.

This program is built around one principle: strength is a running session, not an add-on. Every week pairs your running with targeted strength work aimed at the muscles and patterns that keep runners upright, efficient, and injury-free.
